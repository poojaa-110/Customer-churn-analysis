
# 📊  Telecom Customer Churn Analysis – EDA

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **customer churn in a telecom company** using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

The main objective is to understand **why customers leave the company** and identify customer characteristics and services associated with higher churn.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn can directly affect a company's revenue and growth.

The key business questions explored in this project are:

* What percentage of customers are leaving?
* Which contract types have higher churn?
* Does customer tenure affect churn?
* Which internet services are associated with higher churn?
* Do security and support services influence churn?
* Which payment methods have higher churn?

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains customer information related to:

* Demographics
* Tenure
* Contract type
* Internet services
* Security and support services
* Payment methods
* Monthly and total charges
* Customer churn

**Dataset size:** 7,043 customers and 21 columns.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **26.54% of customers have churned.**
* Month-to-month customers show significantly higher churn than customers with long-term contracts.
* Customers with **shorter tenure** are more likely to churn.
* **Fiber optic customers** show higher churn compared with DSL customers.
* Customers without **Online Security** and **Tech Support** tend to have higher churn.
* **Electronic check** users have the highest churn among payment methods.
* Gender, phone service, and streaming services show relatively weaker relationships with churn.

---

## 📈 Business Takeaways

The analysis suggests that businesses should pay particular attention to:

* New customers with short tenure
* Month-to-month customers
* Fiber optic customers
* Customers without security and support services
* Customers using electronic checks

These customer groups can be considered for **targeted retention strategies and improved customer support**.
